## UniLink Android v1.14.1

- **Fix: the widget Connect/Pause button** now works reliably, opens the Wi-Fi switch when Wi-Fi is off and explains when there is no campus Wi-Fi.
- **New: "Use this account"** on each account card: signs out of the current account, signs in with the chosen one and opens the Connection tab (switch off in Settings). If the chosen account cannot sign in (wrong password, no data left), the Connection tab says why instead of silently using another account.
- **New: "Password rejected" card** on the Connection tab, and a red **Fix password** button on the account.
- **Fix: the battery guide button** did nothing when battery use was already unrestricted; it now opens the app settings page.

### Update
Install `UniLink-v1.14.1.apk` over the previous version; accounts and settings are kept. The signing certificate SHA-256 is at the bottom of these notes (see SECURITY.md).
